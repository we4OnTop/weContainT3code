The target is a precise structured optimum, so the method must find the right basin and then converge fully. Write four methods with different basin-finding ideas. (1) A lattice sweep: hexagonal and square lattices at several orientations, with every choice of removed elements or shifted rows. (2) Concentric rings with systematically varied counts per ring. (3) Greedy gap insertion, followed by remove-the-tightest-and-reinsert moves. (4) Basin hopping from the incumbent, with structured kicks such as a row shift, a ring rotation, or swapping two elements. Use cheap solves to explore, keep a pool of the 3 best distinct candidates, and spend the last 25% of calls on tight-tolerance re-solves of that pool. Treat constraints within 1e-9 of active as active. Stop when the objective changes by less than 1e-13. Never return an infeasible or worse point.
